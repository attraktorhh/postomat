The big idea

Matrix is where you talk, the web is where you look.

Everyone starts in a chat they already use. The web app opens only when something needs a big screen: previews, the calendar, the photo library. Nobody creates an account. The existing Attraktor login (Keycloak) and its groups decide what you can do, in Matrix and on the web.

MI

Mitglied

any member, ~90% of users
  • DMs the bot one sentence
  • answers a few questions
  • gets “ist online” + links
  • sees own posts in “Meine Beiträge”

a member running an event

2–3 people, all in Matrix
  • 👍 in the team room to approve

Caption formula

1Hook as a question or exclamation: “Du wärst gerne kreativ, weißt aber nicht, wie und wo du anfangen sollst…”
2✨ Body, 2–3 short sentences, always “du”
3🕓 Date as “Sonntag, den 18.10.26 | 11 – 13 Uhr” and 📍 Eschelsweg 4
4🇬🇧 English short version (new, so far German only)
5Few hashtags, location tag “Hamburg”
